# Brand

How Helicopter Services looks, sounds and builds pages. Use this page with the components in the sidebar. If something you want isn't here, it probably shouldn't be on the site yet: ask before inventing it.

**In one line:** navy and white, confident and calm, real aircraft and real people, with an orange call to action.

---

## What goes where: anatomy of a page

Every page is built from the same parts, top to bottom. These are the **real components** with real content, not pictures.

> **Header vs Heading:** they're different things.
> - The **site header** is the white bar with the logo and menu at the top of every page.
> - The **page banner** (the `Header` component) is the big photo with the page title.
> - A **Heading** is a section title inside the page.

### Where the words and pictures come from

Most text is edited in **Sanity Studio**. If a field is left empty, the site shows built-in default text, so a page never goes blank.

| On the page | Edit it in Sanity | Field |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero heading and subheading | **Homepage** → Hero | Hero heading, Hero subheading |
| Review score under the hero | **Settings** | Overall review rating, Number of reviews, Review source link |
| Homepage service cards (Training, Flights…) | **Homepage** → Service cards | Title, Description |
| Homepage About / Training / Industry sections | **Homepage** → About, Training, Industry | Tag, Heading, Body, images |
| Homepage flights carousel | **Homepage** → Flights carousel | Pick pages; optional title, text and image |
| Page banner title, tag and photo | The page (**Training / Flights / Industry / Fleet / About**) → Hero | Heading, Tagline, Image (else the page Title and Main image) |
| Page body text and inline images | The page → Body | Rich text |
| "Talk to a pilot" box | The page → Pilot | Pick a Pilot document |
| Helicopters for this service | The page → Helicopters that provide this service | Pick Fleet documents |
| FAQs (FAQ page and Google) | **Settings** → FAQs | Question, Answer |
| Opening hours (header, contact boxes, Google) | **Settings** → Opening hours | Status, Opens, Closes for each day |
| Popup advert | **Popup advert** | Image, dates, link, button text |
| Page titles in Google | Any page → SEO | SEO Title, SEO Description |

**Not in Sanity (ask a developer):** opening hours (`lib/opening-hours.ts`), the menus and footer links (automatic from the pages that exist), the phone number and address, the hero video, and the Tripadvisor reviews on the homepage.

### How much of each

Roughly **60% white or light grey, 30% navy, 10% orange plus light blue**. Orange means "do something here". If everything is orange, nothing is.

### Contrast rules (accessibility)

| Combination | OK? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Navy text on white | ✅ 16:1 | Default for headings |
| White text on navy | ✅ 16:1 | Dark sections |
| Light blue `#1FB6DE` text on white | ❌ 2.4:1 | Known contrast issue, kept as the brand colour |
| Light blue on navy | ✅ 7:1 | Fine for tags and icons |
| White text on orange buttons | ⚠️ 2.9:1 | Fails for small text. Keep button text **bold and 16px+**. Darkening the orange is a design decision still open. |
| Orange text on light grey | ❌ 2.6:1 | Avoid. Put orange links on white. |

## Page rhythm

Pages are stacked full-width bands that alternate backgrounds, so the eye can tell where one topic ends:

| Band | Background | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| Hero or banner | photo/video under 60% navy | `h1`, one line of subheading |
| Section | white | Heading + text + image pair |
| Section | **navy** `bg-brand-dark-blue` | White Heading, light-blue tag, cards |
| Section | **light grey** `bg-brand-light-grey` | Carousel, reviews, FAQ |
| Footer | navy | (automatic) |

- **Spacing:** sections use `py-20`, content sits in `container mx-auto` (2rem side padding).
- **Text width:** body text never runs wider than about 70 characters (`max-w-2xl`).
- **Phones first:** check every page at 390px. Grids collapse to one column (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2`).

## Photography

- **Real aircraft, real people, real places.** The fleet (R22, R44, R66, AS355), White Waltham, London from the air, instructors by name. No stock photos.
- **Show the experience**, not just the machine: a student at the controls beats an empty cockpit.
- **Sizes:**
  - page banners are wide (at least **2400 × 800px**)
  - cards and side images at least **1200px** wide
  - upload originals to Sanity, which resizes them
- **Every image needs alt text** that says what's in it ("Student pilot at the controls of a Robinson R22"). Icons and decoration get empty alt text.

## Writing style

- **British English:** licence, colour, organisation, programme, aeroplane. It's a **hangar**, not a hanger.
- **Plain and confident.** Short sentences. Say what it is, what it costs and how to book.
- **Headlines say what we do** ("Helicopter Trial Lessons & Gifts"), not slogans ("Exceeding exacting standards").
- **Numbers:** "17+ helicopter types" (never "over 17+"), "25 years", "£199", "From £249".
- **Phone:** always written **+44 1494 513 166**.
- **Times:** "8:30am to 5:00pm", UK time. Weekends are "by appointment".
- **Trust signals worth repeating:** UK CAA and EASA approved, operating since 1998, BBC/Channel 4/ITV/Sky credits.
